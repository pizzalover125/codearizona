10 hours of fun, hacking, and workshops!

Code Arizona is a ten-hour event where high-school hackers from across Arizona come together to build apps, games, and much more with the power of code and AI. There are workshops to help you get started, mentors, food, and much more to help you build awesome things at Code Arizona.

Frequently Asked Questions

Any other questions? Send us a message →

What is a hackathon?

Small teams build a project in a fixed amount of time — usually a website, app, game, or some other software thing.

I'm not very good at coding...

That's okay! We'll have workshops teaching web, games, beginner fundamentals, and how to harness AI.

What should I bring?

Bring a laptop, chargers, and a positive mindset!

Will food be provided?

Yes! Food and snacks are provided throughout the event.

How much does entry cost?

Nothing at all. The event is 100% free for all!

Do I need a team to join?

Nope. You'll have time to form teams (max three per team) or work solo.
